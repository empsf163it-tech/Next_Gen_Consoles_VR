Room-Scale Battlestation Setup Checklist

Essential layout practices for maximum tracking fidelity and cable-free movement freedom.

2m × 2m Cleared Volume

Ensure unrestricted arm extension and combat sidesteps without proximity boundary triggers.

📶

Wi-Fi 6E Line of Sight

Place dedicated 160MHz wireless routers 2 meters above floor height facing the primary play volume.

Diffused Ambient Lighting
