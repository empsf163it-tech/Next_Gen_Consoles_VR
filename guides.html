FAST-TRACK SOLUTIONS

Player Hardware & Setup Troubleshooting

Quick answers to common hurdles with wireless link bitrates, controller tracking drift, and cross-platform co-op audio.

How do I eliminate micro-stutter when streaming PCVR wirelessly? +
Connect your PC to your router with a Cat6 Ethernet cable. Use a dedicated 5GHz or 6GHz Wi-Fi 6/6E network for your headset, configure an 80MHz or 160MHz channel width, and cap Virtual Desktop or AirLink bitrate to a fixed 150Mbps to prevent buffer spikes.
Why do inside-out controllers drift during fast room-scale turns? +
Inside-out optical cameras require contrast patterns to calculate position. Avoid pure bare white walls, turn off flickering Christmas LEDs or bright sunlight, and consider an inexpensive 850nm IR illuminator for flawless tracking in dark rooms.
Can I use the PlayStation VR2 headset on PC SteamVR games? +
Yes. With the official PlayStation VR2 PC adapter and a DisplayPort 1.4 connection, you can play the full SteamVR catalog at 2000×2040 per eye with 120Hz refresh rate and full sub-millimeter motion tracking.
